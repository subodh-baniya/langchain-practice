In a world of zeros, ones, and endless code,  
A curious mind begins its road.  
One little line, a simple word,  
A new adventure: **Hello, World!**

A blinking cursor, a screen so bright,  
A thousand dreams come into sight.  
From empty files to things unfurled,  
A tiny program greets the world.

No grand design, no perfect start,  
Just logic, patience, and a curious heart.  
For every genius once preferred,  
To start their journey with "Hello, World!"

And though the code may change with time,  
From simple scripts to systems sublime,  
Remember where your dreams were hurled:  
One little line... **Hello, World!**
